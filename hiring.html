Focus Hub Inc. · Winnipeg, MB

Every story deserves a storyteller who gets it right.

We're a Manitoba non-profit putting cameras, training, and platforms in the hands of Indigenous and newcomer storytellers — so their communities are represented on their own terms.

What we do

Three ways we tell stories

Every role at Focus Hub feeds into one of these — whether you're behind the camera or behind the scenes.

Film & Documentary

Long-form storytelling

Documentary projects that give Indigenous and newcomer voices room to speak in full — not just a soundbite.

Digital Storytelling

Media for right now

Short-form and digital-first content built for the platforms where our communities already are.

Community Training

Passing on the craft

Workshops and mentorship so the people whose stories these are can learn to tell them themselves.
