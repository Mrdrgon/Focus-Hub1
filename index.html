Our Mission

Focus Hub is a non-profit organization dedicated to amplifying the voices and stories of Manitoba's Indigenous peoples and new immigrants through films, documentaries, and digital storytelling.

We aim to preserve and secure identities amongst Indigenous and new immigrant populations, building bridges of understanding through shared narratives.
